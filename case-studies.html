Developing a Portfolio Management Reporting Framework

Situation

When I joined UK Agri-Tech Centre, portfolio reporting was fragmented across multiple spreadsheets, reporting templates and information sources. Project Managers, Project Accountants, Technical Leads and Capability Leads all held different information, making it difficult for senior leadership to obtain a single, accurate view of project status, risks, delivery confidence and upcoming milestones across a large portfolio of commercial, grant-funded and internal projects. This created inefficiencies, increased reporting effort and limited the organisation’s ability to identify emerging delivery risks at portfolio level.

Task

I was tasked with improving portfolio oversight and governance by creating a more effective reporting mechanism that would provide accurate and timely management information, support decision-making, and improve accountability across the project portfolio. I needed to develop a solution that could consolidate information from multiple stakeholders while providing clear visibility of project performance, risks, dependencies and delivery trends.

Action

I designed and implemented a centralised Business Portfolio Review (BPR) framework using Excel. I mapped the reporting requirements of Project Managers, Project Accountants, Technical Leads and Capability Leads, standardised reporting criteria and introduced a consistent RAG methodology across all projects. I developed automated reporting functions that consolidated project schedules, resource information, financial data, risks, issues, actions and project status information into a single portfolio view.

I established governance processes around the reporting cycle, including submission deadlines, review stages and escalation routes. I also created portfolio dashboards showing live projects, projects nearing completion, resource allocation, project values, risk trends and delivery performance. To ensure data quality, I worked closely with stakeholders across the organisation to validate project information and embed ownership of reporting updates.

Result

The new framework provided senior leaders with a single source of truth for portfolio performance and significantly improved visibility of delivery risks, dependencies and upcoming milestones. It enabled earlier identification and escalation of issues, improved the consistency and quality of management information, and strengthened governance across more than 100 projects spanning commercial, grant-funded and internal workstreams. The framework reduced manual effort, improved stakeholder accountability and supported more informed and timely decision-making at both project and portfolio level. Most importantly, it shifted reporting from a reactive exercise to a proactive management tool.

Delivering a Complex Collaborative Innovation Project

GalanCEAmine: Sustainable Galanthamine Production from Daffodils Using Controlled Environment Agriculture · Innovate UK

Situation

At UK Agri-Tech Centre, I managed our contribution to the GalanCEAmine project, an Innovate UK-funded collaborative R&D programme. The project aimed to develop a sustainable, year-round supply of galanthamine, a plant-derived compound used in treatments for Alzheimer’s disease. The consortium involved multiple partners with differing priorities, specialist expertise and delivery requirements, requiring robust governance, stakeholder management and coordination across several work packages.

Task

My responsibility was to ensure UK Agri-Tech Centre’s workstreams were successfully delivered while maintaining compliance with Innovate UK requirements, managing stakeholder relationships, coordinating reporting and governance activities, monitoring risks and dependencies, and ensuring delivery remained aligned with project objectives, milestones and funding conditions.

Action

I established project governance arrangements, coordinated progress reporting and acted as a central point of contact between technical teams, project partners and senior stakeholders. I facilitated regular project meetings, tracked actions, risks, issues and dependencies, and ensured accurate reporting against milestones and deliverables.

To improve oversight, I developed project tracking and reporting mechanisms which provided visibility of progress, resource requirements and emerging risks. I worked closely with technical specialists operating within controlled environment agriculture facilities to ensure outputs remained aligned with project objectives and timelines. Where risks or delivery challenges emerged, I coordinated discussions between partners, challenged assumptions where necessary and ensured agreed mitigation actions were implemented and monitored through governance processes. I also maintained audit trails, managed project documentation and ensured reporting requirements were completed to the standard expected by funders and consortium partners.

Result

The project remained under effective governance, with stakeholders provided with clear, timely and evidence-based information to support decision-making. My approach improved visibility of delivery performance, strengthened communication between partners and ensured risks and dependencies were actively managed. Through structured planning, reporting and stakeholder engagement, I helped support delivery of an innovative programme focused on creating a more sustainable and reliable supply chain for a medicine used in the treatment of Alzheimer’s disease.

Power Statement

“What I’m most proud of is that this wasn’t simply project administration. I was responsible for creating the structure, governance and engagement needed to keep multiple organisations aligned towards a common objective. By providing clear oversight, effective stakeholder management and robust reporting, I enabled informed decision-making and supported delivery of a project with genuine societal, healthcare and sustainability benefits.”

Dancing with Daffodils – Methane Reduction Feed Additive

48-month Innovate UK funded collaborative R&D project

Situation

At UK Agri-Tech Centre, I managed our involvement in the Dancing with Daffodils programme, a 48-month Innovate UK funded collaborative R&D project focused on developing a novel feed additive derived from daffodils to reduce methane emissions from livestock while improving feed efficiency. The project brought together a large consortium of industry, research and agricultural partners, each with different objectives, technical expertise and delivery requirements. The programme supported wider UK ambitions around climate change mitigation, sustainable agriculture and reducing greenhouse gas emissions from the livestock sector.

Task

As Project Manager, I was responsible for coordinating UK Agri-Tech Centre’s contribution to the programme, ensuring project governance, stakeholder engagement, reporting and delivery activities remained aligned with consortium objectives, Innovate UK requirements and agreed milestones. I also needed to provide assurance that risks, dependencies and delivery challenges were identified early and managed effectively.

Action

I established and maintained effective governance arrangements across our workstreams, coordinating regular meetings, tracking milestones, monitoring actions and maintaining accurate project documentation. I worked closely with technical specialists, consortium partners and senior stakeholders to ensure delivery remained on track and that information flowed effectively across organisations.

I produced progress reports, monitored risks and dependencies, challenged delivery assumptions where required and ensured mitigation actions were implemented. Where issues arose, I facilitated discussions between stakeholders to achieve consensus, resolve delivery barriers and maintain momentum. I also ensured compliance with funder requirements, maintained robust audit trails and provided clear reporting to support evidence-based decision making. Throughout the project, I acted as the link between scientific teams, operational stakeholders and senior leaders, translating complex technical information into clear updates and recommendations.

Result

The project continued under strong governance with clear visibility of progress, risks and delivery performance. My coordination helped maintain effective collaboration across a complex consortium while supporting delivery of a project with the potential to reduce agricultural methane emissions, improve livestock productivity and create new opportunities for UK agriculture. Senior stakeholders benefited from clear reporting and robust decision-making information, while delivery teams had improved oversight of milestones, dependencies and project priorities.
